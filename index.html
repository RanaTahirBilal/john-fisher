I
The Classroom

A classroom, then a hymn,
then a book of poems.

John Fisher spent his working life explaining things to people. First science, to elementary and middle school children in Pennsylvania. Then real estate law and practice, to grown adults sitting for a licence exam.

He held the front of a room at the Temple University Real Estate Institute from 2000 to 2016, with a broker's licence in two states behind him. Summers went to Science in the Summer, the programme GlaxoSmithKline ran for children in Philadelphia. Columbia University gave him a book award for what he achieved in education.

In 2016 he founded John W. Fisher Creative Enterprises and started writing in earnest. He has been married fifty-six years. Since 2020 he has been his wife's caregiver, and he writes around it. Both his children became teachers.

The record

Qualified, twice over

Lafayette College — Bachelor of Arts in psychology, cum laude, 1970.
Temple University — Master of Education, then thirty further graduate credits and a supervisory certificate.
Licensed — broker in Pennsylvania and Florida, mortgage certified, green practices certified, and certified to teach elementary and middle school.
Also — coursework at the Institute of Children’s Literature, and articles published in Humpty Dumpty Children’s Digest.

III
His own form

The Sermon Song

A Gospel passage set to music so that a congregation can sing the reading it has just heard preached. He named it, and he trademarked it.

The first full volume drew on twenty-nine sermons given at First Presbyterian Church in Fort Myers between the summer of 2023 and Easter 2024, carrying the Gospel of Mark from the baptism of Christ through to the resurrection. Hymn tunes, folk melodies, children's songs and original settings, whatever the passage asked for.

The concept

Sermon → Song

Notes from a sermon become a setting the congregation can sing the following week. Mark is finished and in print. Acts and Revelation is scheduled for 2026.

The featured work

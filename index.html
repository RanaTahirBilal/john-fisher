I
The Classroom

A classroom, then a hymn,
then a book of poems.

John Fisher spent his working life explaining things to people. First science, to elementary and middle school children in Pennsylvania. Then real estate law and practice, to grown adults sitting for a licence exam.

In 2016 he founded John W. Fisher Creative Enterprises and started writing in earnest.

The record

Qualified, twice over

Lafayette College — Bachelor of Arts in psychology, cum laude, 1970.
Temple University — Master of Education, then thirty further graduate credits and a supervisory certificate.
Licensed — broker in Pennsylvania and Florida, mortgage certified, green practices certified, and certified to teach elementary and middle school.
Also — coursework at the Institute of Children’s Literature, and articles published in Humpty Dumpty Children’s Digest.

At the front of the room

Sixteen years of adults sitting a licence exam.

Before that, a lifetime of children: elementary and middle school science in Pennsylvania, and summers with Science in the Summer, the programme GlaxoSmithKline ran in Philadelphia.

III
His own form

The Sermon Song

A Gospel passage set to music, so a congregation can sing the reading it has just heard preached. He named it, and he trademarked it.

And straightwaycoming up out of the water

Mark 1:10 — sermon, then song

Twenty-nine sermons at First Presbyterian Church, Fort Myers — the Gospel of Mark, baptism through resurrection.

The concept

Sermon → Song

Mark is finished and in print. Acts and Revelation is scheduled for 2026.

Scripture, then setting
